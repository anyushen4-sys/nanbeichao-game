南北朝·天下对弈 (Nan-Bei Chao: War of Dynasties) — 以中国南北朝时代为背景的单机策略卡牌游戏。选择宋、齐、梁、陈、北魏、东魏、西魏、北齐、北周六位/三位历史领袖，统领 63 位史实武将，在三行战场上运筹帷幄、调兵遣将。水墨古风画境，史实考据阵容，每一局都是新的南北之争。

Nan-Bei Chao: War of Dynasties — A single-player strategy card game set in China's Northern and Southern Dynasties (420–589 AD). Lead 63 historically-attested generals under one of nine rulers from Song, Qi, Liang, Chen, Northern Wei, Eastern Wei, Western Wei, Northern Qi, or Northern Zhou. Wage war across three battle lines in this hand-painted ink-wash card game.
